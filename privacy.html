Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CheckAfford.com collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: the numbers you type into our calculators stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

1. Who We Are

CheckAfford.com is a free educational website with loan and debt calculators for people in the United States. It is operated by Elba Nabetcy Guissepe Avalo (“we”, “us”). You can read more on our About page.

2. Information We Collect

Calculator inputs. The financial figures you enter (income, debts, loan amounts, interest rates) are processed entirely in your browser. They are never sent to our servers, and we do not store them.

Usage data. When you visit the site, Google Analytics and Google's advertising technology automatically collect technical and usage information, such as:

  • Browser type and version, and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • Approximate location (country or region level)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Cookie identifiers and similar online identifiers

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Understand how visitors use our tools and articles so we can improve them
  • Monitor site performance and fix technical problems
  • Show advertising that helps pay for keeping the site free
  • Respond to messages you send us

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the figures you enter in the calculators for any purpose.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (property G-ZHN6PQSLWN) to measure traffic and understand which pages are useful. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect the usage data listed above. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.

To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Advertisers do not influence our calculator results or articles, and ads are separate from our editorial content.

6. Affiliate Links

CheckAfford.com does not currently contain affiliate links. If we add them in the future, for example to lending marketplaces, each one will be identified on the page where it appears, and if you click one and complete an application or purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

When you click an affiliate link, the partner may set its own cookies or tracking identifiers so it can credit us. We do not send the figures you entered in our calculators to these partners. What you submit on a partner's site is governed by that partner's privacy policy.

Affiliate relationships do not change our calculator results or the information in our articles.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. On this site they are set by third parties for two purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense and its partners, to serve and measure ads

We do not use cookies to store what you enter in the calculators, and the EN/ES language selector is not remembered between visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site's calculators and articles will keep working.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party websites, including lenders, financial services and affiliate partners. Those sites have their own privacy practices and we are not responsible for them. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

9. Data Retention and Security

Analytics data is kept for no more than 14 months, according to our Google Analytics retention settings. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a reasonable record. Because the calculators run in your browser, the figures you enter never reach our systems. The site is served over HTTPS.

10. Children's Privacy

CheckAfford.com is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, or the EEA and UK), you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising
  • Not be treated differently for exercising these rights

We do not sell personal information. Advertising cookies set by Google may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising under some state laws; you can opt out through the links in Section 5 or by using a browser that sends the Global Privacy Control signal. To make any other request, email us at hello.checkafford@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If you keep using the site after a change, you accept the updated policy.
